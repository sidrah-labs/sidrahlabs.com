Resilient Lakehouse Engineering & Legacy Migrations

We engineer fixed-scope migrations for legacy ERPs, hostile SQL databases, Fivetran-dependent pipelines, and unstructured data systems. By replacing fragile data pipelines with reliable Databricks lakehouse architectures, we eliminate runaway compute costs and silent data corruption.

Pre-Execution Checks & Deterministic Pipelines

Data systems must be safe to change. We do not rely on hope or full-recompute batches. We architect deterministic data pipelines secured by explicit schema contracts, data-recipe rules engines, and pre-execution checks that mathematically catch bad logic edits before they reach your Gold tables.
