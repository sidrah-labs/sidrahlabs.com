Why us

Why data projects stall—and why hiring more people doesn't fix them.

Most teams face the same three problems: AI projects that can't launch because the underlying data is a mess, SaaS pipeline bills that grow every month, and agencies that charge by the hour to build everything from scratch.

  1. 01

    AI fails when nobody trusts the numbers

    Most AI pilots never leave staging because they sit on top of undocumented ERPs and legacy databases. If finance and operations can't reconcile an AI output against the system of record, the project gets shelved.

  2. 02

    Metered SaaS tools tax your growth

    Connector tools like Fivetran charge by the row. As your business grows, your bill climbs for simple data movement that could run inside your own cloud at a fraction of the cost.

  3. 03

    Hourly consulting rewards slow delivery

    Traditional agencies start from a blank repository and bill you to reinvent standard plumbing. Worse, as teams and AI coding tools push changes faster, silent errors slip past basic tests and corrupt executive reports.

Research

Our moat: verifying changes at the speed of AI.

AI has made writing code fast, but trusting code hard. When engineers and AI assistants generate SQL and pipeline changes in seconds, manual review can't keep up—and one bad query can quietly corrupt months of reporting.

Our lab builds verification tools that check data logic automatically. Using mathematical solvers (SMT/Z3) and relational-algebra analysis, we test whether a proposed query or pipeline change can break your business rules before it ever runs.

Why this matters to clients: Every client project shows us real-world failure modes that make our verification tools sharper—and every advance in our lab makes your next deployment faster and safer.
