05 / Applied R&D

Trust the change,
not just the output.

As software and pipeline generation accelerates, the critical bottleneck shifts from writing code to knowing whether a proposed mutation is safe to run.

Our applied research explores AST- and SMT-based pre-execution query verification and deterministic pipeline testing. We believe durable infrastructure tools cannot be theorized in the abstract—they are forged by solving difficult, real-world data problems in production today.
