03

Applied Research

As code generation accelerates, the bottleneck shifts from writing changes to verifying their safety.

Sidrah Labs investigates AST/SMT-based query verification, relational algebra compilation, and symbolic execution harnesses (extending JavaPathFinder and NeuroSPF). Our goal is proving that proposed pipeline transformations cannot execute destructive mutations or corrupt production invariants.
